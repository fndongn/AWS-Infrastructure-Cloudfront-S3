NEW PATIENTS

Your first visit, made simple.

Your consultation is an opportunity to meet our team, discuss your goals, and understand your treatment options.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Three simple steps.

We want you to leave your first visit feeling informed and confident about the next step.

01

Conversation

We'll talk about your goals, concerns, and what you'd like to change about your smile.

02

Evaluation

Our team will perform an orthodontic evaluation and review the factors that influence treatment.

03

Recommendations

We'll explain the available options and answer your questions so you can make an informed decision.

BEFORE YOUR VISIT

A little preparation goes a long way.

Bring your questions

Write down anything you'd like to discuss during your consultation.

Arrive a few minutes early

This gives you time to get comfortable and complete any necessary paperwork.

Come ready to talk

The more we understand your goals, the better we can explain your options.
