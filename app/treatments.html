TREATMENT OPTIONS

A treatment plan that fits your life.

From clear aligners to traditional braces, we offer modern treatment options for children, teens, and adults.

01

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners offer a discreet way to gradually move teeth into healthier alignment. They are removable and designed to fit naturally into everyday routines.

  • Nearly invisible appearance
  • Removable for meals
  • Digital treatment planning
  • Designed for eligible teens and adults
02

Braces

Modern braces provide reliable, predictable tooth movement while offering a variety of options for different treatment needs.

  • Traditional orthodontic brackets
  • Modern low-profile designs
  • Treatment for children, teens, and adults
  • Customized treatment planning
03

Early Treatment

Early evaluations can identify developing orthodontic concerns and help determine whether treatment may be beneficial during childhood.

  • Growth and development evaluation
  • Early orthodontic screening
  • Personalized recommendations
  • Monitoring when immediate treatment isn't needed
